        How the numbers are calculated. List value = quantity × MRP. Net revenue = quantity × MRP × (1 − discount % / 100), with the discount matched on product and channel together. Discount = list value − net revenue. Gross margin = net revenue − quantity × cost price. Orders = distinct order IDs; AOV = net revenue ÷ orders. Suggestions are rule-based and based on only 50 orders, so treat them as directional.
