    How the numbers are calculated. Discount % is matched to each order on product and channel together, since the same product is discounted differently on each channel. List value = quantity × MRP. Net revenue = quantity × MRP × (1 − discount % / 100). Discount = list value − net revenue. Gross margin = net revenue − quantity × cost price. Orders = distinct order IDs; AOV = net revenue ÷ orders.
